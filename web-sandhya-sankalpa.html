Method: Sun by Meeus low-precision solar theory with NOAA sunrise model (upper limb, standard refraction, h = −0.833°); Moon by Meeus ELP-2000 truncated series (60 longitude terms); Drik-ganita with Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa; Chandramana amanta months with adhika/nija detection and Prabhavadi samvatsara changing at Ugadi (Kannada, Telugu, Hindi, English); Sauramana solar months, solar ritus and samvatsara changing at Mesha Sankranti (Tamil default; switchable). Tamil script follows printed sankalpa convention (aspirates written with the base letter). Panchanga for each Sandhya is taken at its anchor: sunrise, solar noon and sunset. Calendar: tithi and nakshatra at sunrise; Pradosham when Trayodashi prevails at sunset; Tamil date by the sunset rule (a sankranti before sunset makes that day the 1st). Rahu kalam, Yamagandam and Gulika are the weekday's fixed eighth of daylight.

Windows: Prātaḥ = 3 ghaṭikas (72 min) before sunrise to sunrise, Aruṇodaya at 96 min before sunrise; Mādhyāhnika = solar noon ± 1 ghaṭika (24 min); Sāyaṃ = 1 ghaṭika before sunset to 2 ghaṭikas after. Printed almanacs (e.g. Ontikoppal) may differ by a minute or two owing to their own computation conventions.
